About rice.ag

Free rice tools, daily rice prices and plain-language rice knowledge.

rice.ag is a free, independent project by people who grow, dry and mill rice in northern Thailand. It has a rice grain scanner, a paddy moisture calculator, daily rice prices and practical guides. Nothing here is for sale.

Why we built it

Most of the rice trade still runs on a quick look at a tray of grains and a trader's word. Laboratory analysis is accurate but slow, and professional grain analysers are expensive. We wanted something in between for our own mill — fast, consistent, and honest about its limits — and saw no reason to keep it to ourselves.

Our principles

  • Free. The tools are free to use, for anyone.
  • Private. Photos are analysed on your device. We don't upload, store or see them.
  • Transparent. We publish how the scanner works, how we test it, and what it can't do.
  • Practical. Everything here is written for people who handle rice, not for search engines.

What's next

  • Validation against hand-sorted and laboratory-graded real samples.
  • Variety presets with reference lengths for common Thai varieties.
  • Fissure (crack) detection and batch scanning for mills.

Where the prices come from

The prices page is rebuilt every day from public sources: the Thai Rice Exporters Association, FAO, the World Bank and Thailand's Department of Internal Trade. Each source is credited and linked on the page.
