How to use it

A answers "how much should I get back?" — enter the wet weight and intake moisture, the moisture you're drying to (usually 14%), and any cleaning or handling loss.

B answers "what was the moisture really?" — enter a drying batch's weight in, weight out and final moisture. If the answer is far above what your meter read at intake, the meter may be reading low on wet paddy. Why this happens and what to do →

Figures are calculated in your browser; nothing is stored or sent. Results are estimates — contracts and official measurements take precedence.
