Urea for rice: when to apply, how much, and what it costs

The Rice Department's recommendations, with today's urea price

Urea (46-0-0) is the cheapest source of nitrogen. In rice it is the top-dressing, applied after the basal fertilizer.

When

  • At maximum tillering
  • At panicle initiation, when the young panicle starts to form

Can I use ammonium sulfate (21-0-0) instead?

Yes. The Rice Department gives 20-30 kg/rai of ammonium sulfate against 10-15 kg/rai of urea, about twice as much, because it is 21% nitrogen against urea's 46%; it also adds sulfur.{% if fg and fg.n %} Right now nitrogen from urea costs {{ '%.1f' % fg.n['46-0-0'] }} baht/kg{% if '21-0-0' in fg.n %} and from 21-0-0 {{ '%.1f' % fg.n['21-0-0'] }} baht/kg{% endif %}.{% endif %}

Don't overdo it

Too much nitrogen makes rice lush and prone to lodging, pests and disease, which costs money without adding yield. If the leaves are already a healthy deep green, the full rate may not be needed.
